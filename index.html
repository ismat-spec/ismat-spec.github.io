About me

I'm a Diploma in AI & Data Engineering student at Nanyang Polytechnic's School of Engineering. What I enjoy most is the unglamorous middle of a data project — validation, storage schemas, detection logic — because that's where a demo either becomes reliable or falls apart.

This year my team built a wearable safety monitor for construction workers, which meant working with live Bluetooth sensor streams, tuning detection thresholds against recorded ground truth, and keeping a database honest at 100 samples per second. On my own time I apply the same pipeline thinking to other domains: training neural networks I implemented myself, backtesting trading strategies over a century of market data, and building interactive tools that make algorithms visible.

I'm working towards roles in data engineering, software engineering and quantitative development.
